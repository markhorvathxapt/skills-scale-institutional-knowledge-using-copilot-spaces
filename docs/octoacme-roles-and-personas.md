# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads oversee architectural decisions and code quality standards. They bridge the gap between management and engineering, ensuring that technical direction aligns with project goals and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define and evolve the technical architecture for the project
- Establish and enforce code quality standards and review practices
- Evaluate technology choices and assess technical trade-offs
- Identify and mitigate technical risks throughout the project lifecycle
- Provide implementation guidance and mentorship to the Development Team

### Goals
- Deliver a scalable, maintainable, and well-documented codebase
- Reduce technical debt and architectural drift
- Ensure that engineering decisions align with business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and design reviews
- Technical feasibility discussions with the Project Manager
- Testing strategy alignment with the QA Lead
- Implementation guidance through code reviews and design sessions

---

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
Product Owners represent stakeholder interests and own the definition of what the team builds. They ensure the backlog is prioritized to deliver maximum business value and that acceptance criteria are clear before work begins.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and refine requirements from stakeholders
- Prioritize and maintain the product backlog
- Define acceptance criteria for features and user stories
- Review and accept completed work against defined criteria
- Resolve scope questions and clarify requirements for the team

### Goals
- Ensure delivered features meet real business and user needs
- Maximize return on investment through clear prioritization
- Reduce rework by providing unambiguous requirements upfront

### Typical Communication
- Backlog grooming and sprint planning sessions
- Acceptance criteria documented in user stories and tickets
- Regular syncs with the Project Manager on scope and timelines
- Feasibility reviews with the Technical Lead

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the testing strategy, quality standards, and release acceptance criteria. They act as the quality gate between development and production, ensuring that software meets reliability and performance expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain the overall testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, regression)
- Define quality thresholds and acceptance criteria in collaboration with the Product Owner
- Manage bug tracking and coordinate resolution with the Development Team
- Establish test automation standards and tooling
- Assess release readiness and communicate quality status to the Project Manager

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Increase test coverage and reduce regression risk
- Provide clear, data-driven release readiness assessments

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test case documentation, and bug reports
- Test coverage reviews with the Technical Lead
- Release quality gate reports for the Project Manager
- Bug triage sessions with the Development Team

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, deployment execution, and rollback procedures. They serve as the central point of accountability for moving software safely and reliably from development to production.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain release schedules and deployment plans
- Coordinate pre-release checklists and go/no-go decision processes
- Work with Operations/Infrastructure teams to ensure environment readiness
- Define and document rollback and incident response procedures
- Communicate release status and any post-release issues to stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal disruption
- Reduce deployment failures through disciplined planning and automation
- Maintain a clear audit trail of release decisions and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Release plans, deployment runbooks, and post-release reports
- Timeline coordination with the Project Manager
- Deployment readiness reviews with the Technical Lead
- Infrastructure and environment briefings with Operations

---

## Stakeholder / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Sponsors define the business objectives and success criteria for a project. They are accountable for funding, high-level scope decisions, and ensuring that the project delivers measurable business value.

### Responsibilities
- Articulate business goals, outcomes, and success metrics for the project
- Approve budgets, resource allocations, and major scope changes
- Review progress and outcomes at key milestones
- Escalate or resolve cross-organizational blockers affecting the project
- Prioritize projects and initiatives within the broader portfolio

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable return on investment
- Maintain visibility into project health and risks
- Make timely decisions to keep the project on track

### Typical Communication
- Executive status summaries and milestone reviews with the Project Manager
- Scope and budget change requests and approvals
- Strategic alignment discussions on project prioritization
